Who We Are

Healthcare that sees the whole person.

At Dr Safeenas Wellness Clinic, healthcare is approached through the connection between medical expertise, rehabilitation and wellness.

With a healthcare legacy of over 30 years, rooted in three hospitals in Kochi, Kerala, India, the clinic brings together doctors, therapists and wellness specialists under one roof.

Our multidisciplinary approach serves women, men and children through a range of healthcare and rehabilitation disciplines.
